# V1.3 Academic Library — Source and Verification Notes

## Verification tiers
- `publisher`: DOI and core metadata verified against publisher page.
- `university`: title, authors, year, journal from official university list. DOI deliberately omitted unless separately verified.
- Existing `draft` / `planned` items are projects, **not** published journal papers.

## Important limitations
- University pages may lag official publication years or use Early Access dates.
- Descriptions for university-only entries are topic summaries based on titles; **not** assertions about empirical results.
- Full-text PDFs are not hosted or reproduced.
- New entries have not all been checked against individual publisher pages.
- The official NSYSU list has 21 journal entries; this release combines its entries with previously existing records and adds separate Nana records.
